About

I work across the whole product process, from discovery to delivery. I run the research and workshops that tell a team what to build first, then stay with designers and developers until it ships.

With 14 years in product design, rooted in multimedia and graphic design, I now lead research, prototyping and design systems for telecom operators, banks and fintechs. I currently lead UX on Orange Maroc's main redesigns.

AI-augmented design

AI in every step

I use AI as a design partner, from the first interview to the coded prototype. It shortens each loop, so teams test more ideas with real users.

Research

AI-assisted synthesis of interviews and feedback into themes, journeys and personas.

Prototyping

Flows, UX copy and coded prototypes generated and refined with AI, linked to Figma.

AI products

ConverseQuery, a generative-AI chatbot, and MLnetworks' AI telecom platform.
