About

I work across the whole product process, from discovery to delivery. I run the research and workshops that tell a team what to build first, then stay with designers and developers until it ships.

With 14 years in product design, rooted in multimedia and graphic design, I now lead research, prototyping and design systems for telecom operators, banks and fintechs. I currently lead UX on Orange Maroc's main redesigns.

Orange Maroc · Telecom & mobile money · Dec 2025 – now

Orange Maroc

Lead UX Designer · research-led redesigns

Problem
Orange Maroc is redesigning its main customer and internal products while building a research-led design practice.
What I did
I lead UX on Max it, Orange.ma, Orange Money (B2C app, B2B app, agents' back-office) and the data dashboards. For the Vue 360 customer view, I led discovery with 12 interviews and 35 survey responses from customer service and sales teams. Across projects I run a research-led method: journey diagnosis, UX benchmark, pain-point prioritisation with the teams, and testing on Maze before development.
Outcome
Discovery identified which customer information service and sales teams need first. Those priorities now drive the Vue 360 redesign.

Crédit du Maroc · Banking · 17 months

MyCDM

Web & mobile banking

Problem
After the acquisition, the platform was hard to navigate, hard to read, and inconsistent: icons, fonts and buttons changed from screen to screen.
What I did
I audited the whole platform and documented each issue with a clear recommendation. I built a design system to keep it consistent, then facilitated workshops to add features and simplify flows such as onboarding and transactions.

New features and flows were tested with users on an interactive prototype before development.
